# Commercial licensing

Traverse has a free, open-source [GPLv3 option](LICENSE). Respawn Studios can also
discuss a separate commercial agreement for rights it controls. You choose the
option that fits your use; a commercial agreement does not cancel anyone's GPL
rights.

## When GPLv3 is enough

You can use Traverse at home or at work, including inside a company. GPLv3 also
allows commercial redistribution. Private internal changes do not, by
themselves, require a public source release. When you distribute covered
software, follow the GPL's source-delivery and other requirements. Those rights
are available without a commercial purchase.

## When to talk to us

An agreement may help when you need permissions beyond GPLv3, such as shipping a
covered derivative under proprietary terms, an OEM arrangement or separately
agreed support. We will review the actual code, dependencies and distribution
plan before making an offer. A platform's own terms still apply; a Traverse
agreement cannot waive someone else's license or platform rules.

Write to [hello@respawn-studios.com](mailto:hello@respawn-studios.com) with a short
description of the intended distribution and the permissions you need. Please
keep confidential technical material out of public GitHub issues.

This page is an invitation to discuss terms, not a grant of commercial rights or
a price list. Additional rights apply only under a separate written agreement
with the relevant rights holder. Third-party components keep their own licenses.
Contributions without the necessary additional permission remain available only
under their existing terms.

Contributing to Traverse does not grant us proprietary relicensing rights.
We do not require a CLA. A commercial proposal involving community contributions
needs permission from their rights holders; we cannot promise those permissions
on their behalf.

## Support is separate

[Supporting Respawn Studios](https://buymeacoffee.com/respawnstudios) is voluntary.
A tip does not purchase a software license, private support, branding permission
or an ownership interest. Any such arrangement needs its own clear terms.
