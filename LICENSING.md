## GPLv3 and commercial permissions

The GPLv3 option remains available to everyone, including companies. It permits
commercial use and redistribution under its terms; private internal use is not
a reason to buy another license. Respawn Studios can negotiate additional
permissions for code it controls, including proprietary distribution, under a
separate written agreement. See [Commercial licensing](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md).
That option does not remove GPL rights or relicense third-party components.

Contributors keep copyright and contribute under GPL-3.0-only, without a CLA or
copyright assignment. This does not give Respawn Studios extra permission to
relicense their work under proprietary terms. Any such permission must come
separately from the relevant rights holders. The [trademark policy](TRADEMARKS.md)
explains names, icons and the identity of unofficial builds separately from
copyright.

## Supporting the project

Support for development is voluntary. It does not buy permission to use,
modify or redistribute Traverse, or unlock application features. Any separate
studio offering must describe its own benefits and must not limit these GPL
rights. Future contributor support-sharing arrangements will have their own
terms; contributing alone creates no payment commitment.

## Matching source for binaries

For public binaries, maintainers must provide matching corresponding source,
including build scripts, resources and non-system dependency sources. A private
engineering source snapshot is not that public deliverable. The
[release-source guide](docs/engineering/OPEN_SOURCE_RELEASE.md) records the
required contents and outstanding launch checks.
