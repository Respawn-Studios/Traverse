# Traverse trademark policy

Traverse, Respawn Studios and the official app icons and logos identify this
project and its publisher. This policy concerns that identity, not your right to
use, study, change or share the software under its copyright license.

## Referring to Traverse

You may name Traverse when discussing it, linking to it, crediting its authors or
truthfully describing a fork. For example, "Based on Traverse" is welcome when
it is clear that your project is independent. Nothing here removes rights that
applicable law gives you, including fair or descriptive use.

You may share an unchanged official release with its existing branding. Keep
its origin clear and do not suggest that your download service is the official
publisher. Personal builds and private modifications do not need rebranding.

## Publishing a fork

Unless Respawn Studios gives written permission, use a distinct app name and
icon for modified or independently compiled public distributions. Choose your
own bundle identifier, signing identity and update feed too. Users should be
able to tell who built a package, who supports it and where its updates come
from. Keep the original copyright and license notices in place.

Do not present a fork, listing, service or merchandise as an official Traverse
or Respawn Studios product, or imply sponsorship or endorsement without
permission. This policy does not claim that these marks are registered in every
jurisdiction, and it does not affect other projects' marks.

## Copyright and commercial permissions

The [GPLv3 copyright license](LICENSE) grants no separate trademark license.
Copyright permission for an icon or other resource does not grant permission to
use it as confusing product branding. A [commercial agreement](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md)
also needs to address branding explicitly if you want to use the official marks.
